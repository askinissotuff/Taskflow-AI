# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in TaskFlow AI to be welcoming and respectful to everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on ideas and work, not personal attacks.
- Assume good intent while being open to correction.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's personal information.
- Keep discussions relevant and welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior in this project, contact the repository owner privately through the contact options on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and respectfully as possible.

## Scope

This applies to project spaces and interactions where someone is representing or participating in TaskFlow AI.
